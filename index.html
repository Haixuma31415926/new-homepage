Haixu Ma 马海旭

Senior Research Scientist at Meta

My work focuses on LLM post-training and agentic AI, spanning reinforcement learning, on-policy distillation, non-linear agentic reasoning, and self-improvement loops for long-horizon tasks. I also work on developing large-scale AI production systems, including self-improving agentic recommendation systems at Meta.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, advised by Prof. Yufeng Liu and Prof. Donglin Zeng. My Ph.D. research focused on causal inference, reinforcement learning, and statistical machine learning for personalized decision making. I earned my B.S. in Statistics from Nankai University.
